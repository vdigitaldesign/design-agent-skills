# Native design records and delivery

Use this reference with the Claude native-record skills. Read it once for the chain and reuse the current text at later steps. It defines authority and delivery invariants, not an additional authoring form. Use the headings and files that serve the commissioned result.

## Sources, scope and authority

Keep product facts, effective product/system canon, approved human choices, proposed changes, observed evidence and lineage distinct. The authoritative source owns its rule; a contract or brief may clearly state that rule with its exact owner/revision, without changing its meaning or acquiring separate authority. Make the full controlling text accessible unchanged, inline or in included source files. An ID, summary, prototype, validation result or index is not a substitute for its controlling text.

Use the supplied authority and scope. Follow stricter consumer policy; otherwise an explicit scoped instruction or selection from the user commissioning the task is valid authority for that choice. Silence, reactions, vague approval, an agent recommendation and approval of another revision supply no new authority. Preserve exact approved text, protected boundaries and real permissions. A material change to an approved proposal needs renewed approval for its exact scope.

Material choices change user outcomes, flows, information architecture, routes, content meaning, data/fields, permissions, failure/recovery, accessibility, responsive meaning, identity, system posture or selection between substantive alternatives. Routine conformance applies an already effective rule without new meaning: return the concise native handoff under that rule, without a new contract, exploration, selection or effectivity gate.

Before implementation of material design, the controlling contract or canon must actually be effective for that scope. Use record-specific statuses:
- Decisions: proposed / approved / rejected / superseded / revoked.
- Contracts: draft / review-ready / effective / superseded / revoked.
- System canon: proposed / review-ready / effective / superseded / revoked. Effective canon needs exact approval and actual consumer-owned publication.

Validation supports review-ready; it grants neither effectivity nor publication. Publishing a system pattern does not make a separate contract effective. An approved visual/motion choice supplies decision evidence, not implementation authority. A projection never gains source authority.

Resolve applicability using original source conditions and scope. Prefer the most specific current authoritative source within its scope and apply supplied precedence/supersession where it governs; preserve unresolved material conflicts with their source revisions and dependent scope rather than silently selecting a winner. Inspect permitted relevant evidence and finish independent work. A genuine missing fact, selection, authority, tool or permission stops only its dependent action. Give established findings, viable options, a supported recommendation/trade-offs and the smallest irreducible question. Continue the safe chain in the same turn.

Evidence depth follows actual reach: routine, bounded material or high-impact. Cross-surface reach, irreversibility, uncertainty, migration or multiple authorities require deeper relevant evidence, compatibility and affected-consumer checks. The depth does not impose a universal risk score or extra document set.

Engineering-only work with no design consequence passes to engineering. Observing API/database/operational evidence does not activate a mutation or specialist workflow; stricter consumer routing still applies. Re-enter design when product behavior, content meaning, accessibility or effective design rules change.

## Native result and requested views

Author each material choice, requirement, scope, unknown, gate, acceptance procedure and verdict at its native owner once. The complete contract/proposal and its validation are the working result. Required briefs, coding packets and coordination records consume that same content, rather than assigning another behavior specification.

When the native acceptance index owns executable procedures, use [executable acceptance](executable-acceptance.md) for artifact identity and evidence scope. The handoff carries those exact files or accessible unchanged locators, their tested hashes and actual limitations. Setup/actions/assertions remain at that native artifact; projections carry its meaning without authoring a second procedure. Concrete checks outside the runtime's capabilities remain present with their own source-backed observation requests and status.

Use ordinary authorized file operations to copy exact owned blocks/values or link exact accessible native sections. Choose a single file with separately identified records or multiple files as useful. Give each view its identity/revision, exact source revisions, generated-at date and limitations. Presentation labels and observed revision/hash/time metadata may differ; a copied view supplies no new behavior, permission, scope or approval. Read reproduced content back, check its revision and links in the unit actually delivered, and keep full controlling sources available. A tool-written reinterpretation is new authorship, not copying.

A requested human brief exposes the material question, scoped authority/protected boundaries, actual evidence/limitations, comparable options/artifacts, recommendation/trade-offs/risks/falsifying evidence and exact selection/effectivity request. Take these from the current native proposal/decision and relevant contract/validation, pinned to their exact revisions and, when carried, the source packet revision. Do not fill a separate inventory merely to create the brief.

A material coding handoff carries the scoped outcome, controlling sources/IDs/revisions/status, actual selection/effectivity/publication references, complete acceptance checks and evidence request, protected boundaries/freedoms, observed artifacts/limitations, applicable parity environments and real return conditions. Its source coverage includes relevant behavior/state/content/a11y/responsive/localization/RTL/theme conditions. Pin the native contract and any carried packet revision. Until every required normative source is effective, label the draft NOT AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION and name the exact pending gate and dependent scope. These meanings remain required even when combined into the native result.

The routine handoff retains exactly these six concise labels: Controlling revision; Observed delta; Exact correction; Protected boundaries; Acceptance and parity request; Return to design. Request the smallest needed implementation evidence; no full future environment matrix is required before evidence exists.

When visual, motion or external assets are in scope, carry their approved meanings and eligibility with the native result. A material Motion Choice Record cites its exact Owner Decision Record and authority/scope; effective sources supply implementation requirements, including purpose, triggers/affected states, timing/sequence/interruption/completion, keyboard/touch/non-hover operation, reduced/non-motion fallback, performance/lifecycle, dependency/provenance/license and actual validation evidence/limitations. No motion quota applies. Asset records carry the provider/locator, provenance/license and consumer rights review, production eligibility, remote/local state, fallback/crop, alt-text owner, limitations and any replacement before shipping. Remote/reference material is not automatically production-eligible. Only eligible assets enter an implementation-authorized packet; prototype replacements block their dependent shipping scope. An optional Visual System Projection covers applicable identity/authority, visual premise, color/type/spacing/shape/depth, components/states, layout, motion/fallbacks, imagery/provenance, required/allowed/forbidden/non-fit choices and genuine unresolved items. It stays non-normative, source-pinned and NOT AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION when based only on a non-effective decision. Regenerate it from the changed native source.

## Carried coordination and freshness

Use a supplied valid Design Work Packet without reconstructing intake or rereading pinned authority merely to confirm it. This native route returns the commissioned records without automatically creating another packet. If the commission or a supplied consumer interface requires a packet, carry one identity and record its actual facts; no separate authoring exercise is added.

A carried packet remains valid when scope matches, required fields are present or explicitly unknown, source status/freshness is usable, conflicts are visible, and the sequence/next gate are current. Refresh the same identity only for a missing required field (rather than an explicitly recorded unknown), stale/unavailable/conflicting required source, changed scope/authority, or genuinely new evidence required by the downstream task. Record changed fields, reason, evidence and revision. These bounds apply at each native skill; a new stage alone triggers neither refresh nor a fresh packet.

Keep actual packet meanings when one is required: identity/revision/tier/time/custodian; commissioned scope/deliverable; authority/materiality/permissions; source/evidence revisions and limitations; facts/inferences/unknowns/conflicts with inspected evidence boundary; protected boundaries; native record locators/status; active/current/inactive workflow scope and reasons; completed work/findings; exact next gate/authority/dependent scope; issued view revisions. Stage updates carry its native record, actual gate changes and next safe step; they do not independently reinterpret the source.

Before using a view, resolve source revision/status/freshness and compare meaning and authority with the current native owners. Missing, added, stale or contradictory content remains visible. Correct meaning at its native owner and recheck affected content, then refresh only affected views after corrections or real authority events. A missing recording value is a task for that native author, not itself a missing product fact. An incomplete copy/readback is not a completed handoff; byte equality alone never proves semantic correctness.

In design-surface findings use implementation discrepancy / contract gap / design debt / product question / inconclusive observation. In implementation-parity requests use implementation defect / contract gap / inconclusive observation / proposed material delta requiring owner approval. Parity evidence cannot change effective requirements or approve a delta. Planned verification, adoption or implementation is distinct from observed completion.
