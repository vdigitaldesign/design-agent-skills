# Reconstruct Design Context

Create an evidence-backed Design Read and advance every part of the design task that does not depend on an irreducible missing fact.

## Packet mode

- **Carried:** use and update the supplied valid Design Work Packet. Do not reconstruct its intake or reread pinned authority merely to confirm it.
- **Direct single-skill:** when the Design Read is the complete requested outcome, return it without creating a packet.
- **Direct expansion:** when direct reconstruction becomes a material or uncertain multi-skill chain, create exactly one [Design Work Packet](../references/packet-projections.md#design-work-packet) before the first downstream handoff.

Refresh a carried packet only when a required field is missing, a source is stale or conflicting, scope or authority changes, or the task genuinely needs new evidence.

Without a valid packet, read [authority and status](../references/authority-and-status.md). Use the [Design Read template](../references/task-context-templates.md) only when a full durable Design Read is requested or the missing context cannot be represented compactly in the packet. Load the whole [Product Design Profile and tool-neutral evidence contract](../references/product-profile-and-tools.md) only when consumer mappings, destinations, privacy, evidence methods, external-reference capture, or connected retrieval affect the work.

When external references are material, create the central Reference Capture Receipt from that same reference. Validate collection permission and boundaries before access, then use its lowest-sufficient-evidence ladder. If live or static evidence is unavailable, continue with the next permitted supplied artifact or ask for the smallest bounded human evidence. Preserve `partial`, `unavailable`, or `inconclusive` status. Do not fabricate coverage.

## Source order

Inspect the relevant sources available within the task’s permissions. Prefer, within their scopes:

1. effective product canon and Design Contracts with their statuses recorded;
2. approved decision records and consumer policy;
3. authoritative platform or design-system rules;
4. product research, support, analytics, and content sources;
5. observed interface behavior and implementation evidence;
6. secondary references and agent inference.

Recency does not override authority automatically. Record conflicts instead of silently choosing a convenient source.

Resolve exact known source IDs first. Prefer path- or collection-scoped retrieval. Inspect bounded match counts before you broaden a search for an ambiguous noun. Limit returned records, fields, and excerpts. Expand only when the bounded evidence is insufficient. Record retrieval scope, count, insufficiency, and expansion in the carried packet.

Reading an API, data, or database contract as evidence does not activate an operational workflow unless mutation, implementation, diagnosis, or consumer policy requires it. Always follow stronger consumer routing.

## Build the Design Read

For each material claim, record the claim, class, source, authority, scope, confidence, freshness, and consequence:

- **Fact:** directly supported by a source authoritative for that claim.
- **Inference:** a reasoned interpretation, with its evidence and uncertainty.
- **Unknown:** information not established by the permitted sources.
- **Conflict:** two claims that cannot both govern the same scope.

Also record observed surfaces and states, evidence capture method and time, product/profile scope, protected boundaries, permissions, privacy limitations, debt or exceptions, and material decisions requiring approval.

## Advance before escalating

Continue safe work such as inventory, source comparison, state mapping, audit, reuse discovery, and outlining decision options. Escalate only when a missing fact or authority would materially change the next dependent step.

When you escalate, provide findings, viable options, trade-offs, and the smallest question that lets work resume. Include a recommendation when justified. If evidence cannot support a direction, abstain explicitly before exploration. Do not generate confident UI without that evidence.

## Boundaries

- Observation does not become product canon.
- Token or style frequency and a single screenshot are observations, not proof of semantic roles, system health, motion intent, or hidden canon.
- An adapter or packet may own mapping, observation, or lineage metadata but not duplicate the facts it references.
- A self-contained Design Read, contract, or handoff may concisely restate an approved fact when it cites the single normative owner and source revision. The restatement gains no independent authority.
- Do not invent “realistic” users, dates, logos, legal copy, metrics, testimonials, or product rules.
- Do not resolve protected-boundary or authority conflicts by aesthetic preference.

In carried or direct-expansion mode, update the same packet ID with:

- the current step;
- the Design Read locator and revision;
- new and resolved facts/inferences/unknowns/conflicts;
- exact source lineage;
- findings to preserve;
- the active sequence;
- the next gate and stop.

In direct single-skill mode, return the native Design Read without a Design Work Packet. Continue the safe chain unless a genuine blocker remains.
