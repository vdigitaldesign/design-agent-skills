# Evolve Design System

Turn an approved system posture into the smallest concrete, reviewable canon proposal. Write the actual proposed design-system change, with enough context for a human to decide and an affected consumer to adopt it. Use a natural format appropriate to the change; no full inventory or fixed proposal form is required.

Read [native design delivery](../references/native-design-delivery.md) once for this chain. Reuse its already-read current copy at later stages. It supplies the authority, status, packet-validity and refresh rules and the rules for commissioned copied views; it does not impose another proposal-authoring form.

## Establish the scope

Use the evidence-backed assessment, exact scope, current effective canon or its confirmed absence, protected boundaries, and approved posture. Follow stricter consumer policy; otherwise an explicit scoped instruction or selection from the user commissioning the task is valid posture authority. Approval of a posture authorizes drafting, not effectivity of the resulting text.

Evidence-backed `consume` needs no system proposal: proceed under the effective canon. If the posture is materially undecided, complete safe analysis and candidate drafting, then present the findings, viable options, recommendation, trade-offs, and bounded human choice. Do not silently choose a posture.

## Produce the proposed change

Author one native proposal with exact proposed text, relevant alternatives and recommendation, trade-offs, preserved behavior, affected scope and consumers, acceptance checks, adoption or verification information, unresolved material questions, and the exact human decision requested. Keep its identity, revision, and authority distinct from any Design Contract.

- **Extend:** add the missing token relationship, component, or pattern contract, states, usage guidance, and acceptance checks while preserving the healthy base.
- **Repair:** correct the deficient rule, identify affected states and exceptions, and define consumer adoption and verification.
- **Constrain:** define the reliable subset, prohibited or unreliable uses, temporary exceptions, debt owner, and exit condition.
- **Establish:** create only the coherent foundations, tokens, component or pattern contracts, states, documentation, and ownership needed for this scope.
- **Replace-migrate:** define target canon, compatibility boundary, migration stages, deprecation and final removal conditions, exception handling, verification, and rollback.

Specify the layers this change actually affects. For tokens and themes, explain semantic purpose, relationships, aliases/fallbacks, mode or platform limits, and affected consumers. For components, cover purpose, anatomy, variants, sizing/content/layout, composition, and implementation freedoms. For patterns, cover component relationships, task sequence, hierarchy, permissions, partial outcomes, and recovery. Retain all applicable source requirements, including behavior, content, full states, keyboard/focus/semantics, responsive, visual relationships, contrast/theme, localization/RTL, platform, and motion; do not turn an unresolved product choice into a component variant or API.

Make the resulting rule retrievable and usable: give its scope and identity, source relationships, use/avoid/known non-fit, applicable examples or counterexamples, acceptance and parity evidence, owner, status/revision, and review or freshness triggers. Include exceptions, debt, drift controls, fixtures, migration, supersession and adoption-receipt needs where the change requires them. Preserve product, legal, privacy, analytics, and localization authority. Do not create a complete token universe for one need.

Keep the proposal in Markdown and technology-neutral unless approved consumer constraints require otherwise. Do not implement code, framework APIs, a registry, schema engine, or package runtime.

## Check and hand over

Check source and authority lineage, consistency, accessibility and all applicable states, affected consumers, migration risk, acceptance checks, and protected boundaries. Apply non-material corrections. Mark the proposal `proposed` or `review-ready`; expose material questions rather than deciding them silently. Present the exact change, preserved behavior, risks, trade-offs, adoption/migration impact, evaluation and verification plan to the human effectivity authority. The agent cannot make it effective.

Required briefs and handoff copy or locate this proposal's meaning, not a second version. For a carried packet, update its current step, proposal locator/revision/status, posture authority, findings, resolved posture gate, effectivity gate, active sequence, next step, and stop from native records and actual events; preserve the existing source context. Direct proposal-only work returns the proposal without creating a packet; genuine downstream expansion follows the shared delivery rule.

After explicit human approval of the exact proposal under applicable policy, route to [publish-design-system-canon](publish-design-system-canon.md) for durable consumer-owned publication, revision/supersession lineage, retrieval metadata, and adoption receipts.
