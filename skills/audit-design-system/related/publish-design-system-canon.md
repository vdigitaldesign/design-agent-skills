# Publish Design-System Canon

Close the gap between an approved proposal and retrievable, durable consumer-owned canon without creating a second source of truth.

## Packet mode

- **Carried:** use and update the supplied valid Design Work Packet. Do not reconstruct its intake or reread pinned authority merely to confirm it.
- **Direct single-skill:** when exact canon publication is the complete requested outcome, return its native publication/adoption record without creating a packet.
- **Direct expansion:** when direct publication becomes a material or uncertain multi-skill chain, create exactly one [Design Work Packet](../references/packet-projections.md#design-work-packet) before the first downstream handoff, using the [governed-view assembly](../references/packet-projections.md#governed-view-assembly) from the existing sources and native records.

Refresh a carried packet only when a required field is missing, a source is stale or conflicting, scope or authority changes, or genuinely new evidence appears.

Without a valid packet, read [authority and status](../references/authority-and-status.md). Load [design-system craft and publication](../references/design-system-craft.md). Load [packet projections](../references/packet-projections.md) when publishing or refreshing a projection. Load the whole consumer [Product Design Profile](../references/product-profile-and-tools.md) when destination, permissions, privacy, consumer mapping, or verification environment is not already established by current authoritative evidence.

## Preconditions

Require:

- the exact change-proposal ID and revision;
- explicit human effectivity approval whose authority covers that scope;
- current canon and supersession targets;
- consumer-owned canonical destination, ownership, permissions, and privacy rules;
- affected consumers and required adoption/migration evidence.

Selection of a direction or posture approval alone is insufficient. Silence, a reaction, “looks good,” or approval of a different revision is insufficient.

Before you declare publication unavailable, check the capabilities and permissions of the tools available for the authorized destination. A read-only host shell does not establish that a separately provided workspace or destination tool is read-only. Use an available authorized write route. Do not bypass a denied permission or invent an unprovided route.

When no permitted write route is available, or an attempted authorized write fails without a permitted alternative, produce a ready-to-publish record with the observed blocker. Do not claim publication.

## Publish the effective revision

Use ordinary authorized file operations for the already approved body and lineage materialization. Copy its exact operative text; record new authority only from the actual scoped human approval, and publication only from the observed write/readback. Receipts and packet/projection updates copy these real events and native records. This assembly does not interpret a new product rule, infer contract effectivity, or replace any precondition or publication check below.

1. Verify that the approved text matches the proposal revision. Present any material difference for renewed approval.
2. Write or revise the consumer-owned Markdown canon at its authorized destination. Include normative rules, owner, scope, revision, status, effective date, approval reference, acceptance criteria, implementation freedoms, lineage, and retrieval terms.
3. Preserve history. Mark the displaced revision `superseded` with a forward link, or record authorized revocation and safe fallback. Never erase the revision that governed prior decisions.
4. Link related Product Design Profile, protected boundaries, Design Contracts, exceptions, debt, and evaluation fixtures without copying their normative facts.
5. Update or propose the consumer’s Design Knowledge Index entry so later tasks retrieve the current effective revision and known non-fit.

Materialize the publication record from unchanged approved facts or their exact accessible owner/revision locators, plus the observed publication/approval events. Do not independently compose another interpretation or expand the approved scope.

Confirm publication through an available authorized tool. Read the destination back and check that its content and revision match the approved proposal, including any approval/effectivity metadata. A record printed in the response, a planned write, or a tool call without a confirmed result is not evidence of publication. If the write succeeds but read-back is unavailable, distinguish the observed write result from the unverified content. Do not claim full verification.

An optional Visual System Projection may accompany publication only when it is generated from that same exact published revision and cites it. It remains non-normative. When canon changes, regenerate or revise the projection and its lineage. Never edit the projection as if it were the canonical change.

## Adoption and migration

Create an Adoption or Migration Receipt for each consumer or bounded rollout group that was actually observed. Record prior rule, target revision, status, evidence, exceptions, debt, verification result, and rollback state. A planned receipt is not adoption evidence.

For replace-migrate or broad repair work, preserve compatibility boundaries, staged adoption, deprecation, exception handling, verification, rollback, and final removal conditions from the approved proposal.

## Retrieval and continuation

Return the canonical locator, effective revision/status, approval and supersession lineage, knowledge-index locator, affected consumers, receipt status, and next verification action. Design and coding agents can use an effective revision immediately within its scope. Consumers still require implementation and parity evidence.

Continue to dependent Design Contract or handoff work in the same turn when authority and sources are complete. Publishing design-system canon does not make a separate draft Design Contract effective and does not authorize code changes by itself.

In carried or direct-expansion mode, mechanically materialize the following values from the actual approval, published canon and observed receipt; preserve independent contract effectivity:


- the current step;
- the published canon locator, revision, and status;
- supersession and approval lineage;
- projection, index, and adoption receipt locators;
- findings to preserve;
- the resolved effectivity gate;
- the remaining adoption or contract gate;
- the active sequence;
- the next step and stop.

In direct single-skill mode, return the native publication output without a Design Work Packet.
