# Maintain Design Knowledge

Keep cross-task design knowledge retrievable, current, and subordinate to its authoritative sources.

## Packet mode

- **Carried:** when invoked from a carried chain, use and update the supplied valid Design Work Packet. Do not reconstruct its intake or reread pinned authority merely to confirm it.
- **Direct single-skill:** when a knowledge query or index update is the complete requested outcome, return the native result without creating a packet.
- **Direct expansion:** when direct knowledge work becomes a material or uncertain multi-skill chain, create exactly one [Design Work Packet](../references/packet-projections.md#design-work-packet) before the first downstream handoff.

Refresh a carried packet only when a required field is missing, a source is stale or conflicting, scope or authority changes, or genuinely new evidence appears.

Without a valid packet, read [authority and status](../references/authority-and-status.md). Load the [Design Knowledge Index](../references/design-knowledge-index.md) for query, capture, freshness, rejection, or supersession work.

## Entry boundary

Require a concrete retrieval need or evidence that the learning can materially improve later tasks across scopes. Inputs may come from an audit, Design Lab, visual direction, reuse assessment, contract, publication, or parity record. Product-private facts, one-off observations, generic model knowledge, and unverified preferences remain in their task records.

Confirm the authorized index destination and permission to maintain its metadata. The index may own provenance, retrieval, freshness, lifecycle, applicability, and lineage metadata. It never owns or copies the product/design-system facts it links.

## Query and retrieval

1. Query by behavior, state, constraint, platform, product need, and known non-fit.
2. Resolve the primary source, exact revision, freshness, lifecycle, consumer mapping, and evidence boundary.
3. Return the best applicable current entry, disclose a bounded fallback, or abstain after one broadened query when no responsible match exists.
4. Carry the entry/source revision into the consuming Reuse Assessment and, when packet mode applies, the Design Work Packet. A retrieved entry cannot override stricter consumer canon.

## Capture or update

Create the smallest index entry that changes future retrieval. Record:

- use, avoid, and known non-fit;
- provenance/rights;
- authoritative locator and revision;
- evidence boundary;
- applicability;
- freshness trigger;
- aliases;
- lifecycle;
- consumer mappings;
- evaluation;
- metadata maintainer.

Summarize only enough to retrieve and apply the source responsibly. Link effective canon rather than reproducing its normative text. If the source cannot be cited or reused at the needed level, preserve the restriction and do not synthesize a competing rule.

## Freshness, rejection, and supersession

- **Freshness review:** verify the source and consumer mapping. Mark current, review-due, stale, or withdrawn with evidence and date.
- **Reject/non-fit:** retain the candidate and reason when it is misleading, unsupported, unsafe, or inapplicable so future retrieval does not rediscover it as a recommendation.
- **Supersede:** keep the historical entry, point it to the replacement, and state which scopes move to the new revision. Never use last-write-wins or erase prior decision lineage.
- **Canon conflict:** update index metadata and surface the conflict. Do not silently move a consumer from its effective pinned canon.

## Output and continuation

Return the query result or exact Markdown entry/revision, changed lifecycle/freshness fields, source lineage, known non-fit, affected retrieval terms, consumer-canon conflicts, and next review trigger. Continue the originating design workflow with the retrieved evidence when safe.

Do not force an index mutation on every task. Capturing reusable learning is complete only when the authorized durable destination is actually updated. Otherwise, return a ready entry and the exact destination/permission blocker without claiming publication.

In carried or direct-expansion mode, update the packet with:

- the current step;
- the knowledge query/index locator and revision;
- retrieved or changed metadata;
- source lineage;
- originating findings to preserve;
- conflicts or freshness gates;
- the return workflow;
- the next step and stop.

In direct single-skill mode, return the native knowledge result without a Design Work Packet.
