# Publish Design-System Canon

Publish the approved text at the consumer's authorized canonical destination and return a native publication/adoption record that lets the next person retrieve and use it. This work materializes an actual approved change; it does not design another version of that change.

Read [native delivery and authority](references/native-design-delivery.md) once per chain; reuse it when its current text is already in context. Its authority, packet validity/refresh and governed-view rules apply to this work. Use the supplied sources, exact approval, current canon and native decision/contract records. Obtain destination, permissions, privacy, ownership, consumer mapping and verification constraints from current authoritative evidence; consult the relevant consumer policy or profile only for a fact that is not already established. Do not load a separate common authoring template or reconstruct complete profile/intake forms just to publish this revision.

## Establish what may be published

Confirm the exact proposal ID/revision and operative text, the human's effectivity authority for that scope, the current canon and supersession targets, the consumer-owned canonical destination and its ownership/permission/privacy rules, and the affected consumers and required adoption or migration evidence.

An approved direction or posture authorizes drafting, not publication of an unapproved revision. Silence, a reaction, “looks good,” or approval of a different revision does not provide exact effectivity authority. A material difference from the approved text needs renewed approval. Missing authority or a real conflict blocks the dependent publication; finish independent authorized work and identify the smallest fact or decision needed.

Before declaring publication unavailable, check the capabilities and permissions of the available tools for the authorized destination. A read-only host shell does not prove a separately supplied workspace or destination tool is read-only. Use an available authorized route, but do not bypass a denied permission or invent an unprovided route. If no permitted route exists, or an authorized write fails without a permitted alternative, return the exact ready-to-publish text and observed blocker. Do not label it published.

## Materialize and confirm the revision

Use ordinary authorized operations to write or revise consumer-owned Markdown. Copy the approved operative body exactly. Carry its normative rules, owner, scope, revision, status, effective date, approval reference, acceptance criteria, implementation freedoms, lineage and retrieval terms from the approved material or its exact accessible owner/revision locators. Record approval and publication events separately from the body; do not invent missing facts, expand scope, or independently re-author those values in another form.

Preserve history. Mark a displaced revision `superseded` with a forward link to the effective replacement. For authorized revocation, record the safe fallback or its absence. Never erase the revision that governed prior decisions. Preserve related consumer Product Design Profile, protected-boundary, Design Contract, exception, debt and evaluation-fixture locators without copying their normative facts into new owners.

Update the consumer's Design Knowledge Index entry when authorized, or provide the exact proposed update when the index cannot yet be changed. Its retrieval metadata must identify the current effective revision, lineage and known non-fit. Keep an index update proposal distinct from an observed index write.

Read the destination back through an available authorized tool and compare its content, revision and approval/effectivity metadata with the exact approved material. A response containing the text, a planned write, or a tool call with no confirmed result is not publication evidence. If a write succeeded but readback is unavailable, record the observed write and unverified content separately; do not claim full verification. Canon is `effective` only with exact scoped approval and publication at its consumer-owned destination, with any verification limitation explicit.

An optional Visual System Projection may accompany publication only as a non-normative view of the same exact published revision, with its source citation. If canon changes, revise or regenerate the projection and its lineage. Never edit it as the canonical change.

## Record actual adoption and migration

For each consumer or bounded rollout group actually observed, create a native Adoption or Migration Receipt recording the prior rule, target revision, status, evidence, exceptions, debt, verification result and rollback state. A planned rollout or planned receipt is not adoption evidence.

For replace-migrate or broad repair, carry the approved compatibility boundaries, staged adoption, deprecation, exception handling, verification, rollback and final removal conditions unchanged. Publication alone does not prove consumer implementation, migration or parity.

## Finish the handoff

Return the canonical locator and effective revision/status, exact approval and supersession lineage, index/projection/receipt locators and their observed or pending state, affected consumers, findings to retain and the next verification action. Use an ordinary native record; do not fill unused inventory, gap or packet forms. These locators and events must remain accessible to the recipient.

If a valid work packet was carried, update it from these native records under the shared delivery/freshness rules. Do not reconstruct its intake or reread pinned authority without a refresh trigger. A direct chain returns its commissioned native records; use the shared rules for a required packet, brief or execution view. Copy the actual current step, canon revision/status, approval and supersession lineage, index/projection/receipt locators, preserved findings, resolved effectivity gate, remaining adoption or contract gate, active sequence, next step and stop; do not independently reinterpret them.

Continue dependent contract or handoff work in the same turn when sources and authority are complete. Design and coding agents may use the effective canon within its scope; consumers still need implementation and parity evidence. Canon publication neither makes a separate draft Design Contract effective nor authorizes code changes. Keep its independent effectivity and any remaining real gate explicit.
