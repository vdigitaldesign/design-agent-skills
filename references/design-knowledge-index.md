# Design Knowledge Index

Use this durable Markdown index when a design mechanism, trusted source, system item, or known non-fit should be retrievable across tasks. [`maintain-design-knowledge`](../skills/maintain-design-knowledge/SKILL.md) owns the capture, query, freshness, rejection/non-fit, and supersession workflow.

Store links, metadata, decisions, and evidence boundaries. Never store copied product canon or a competing source of truth.

## Index entry

- **ID and title:** stable, descriptive, and scoped.
- **Aliases and retrieval terms:** product language, common synonyms, and related capability IDs.
- **Knowledge type:** product pattern / design-system item / platform guidance / trusted library / research finding / design technique / failure pattern.
- **Use when:** conditions and product needs the item supports.
- **Avoid when:** risks, authority conflicts, or contexts where it should not be applied.
- **Known non-fit:** tested or evidence-backed cases where the item fails or becomes misleading.
- **Source authority:** primary owner, canonical URL/path, exact revision/pin, and locator.
- **Provenance and rights:** author/publisher, license or citation boundary, attribution, and restrictions relevant to reuse.
- **Evidence boundary:** what the source or example does and does not prove.
- **Applicability:** platforms, products, states, audiences, constraints, and design-system dependencies.
- **Freshness:** verified date, review trigger, current / review-due / stale / withdrawn.
- **Lifecycle:** proposed / trusted for retrieval / superseded / rejected; supersedes and replacement links.
- **Consumer mappings:** Product Design Profile or canon references that permit local use; no copied facts.
- **Evaluation:** fixtures, observed outcomes, counterevidence, and unresolved questions.
- **Maintainer and change note:** metadata owner and reason for the latest revision.

## Retrieval procedure

1. Query by product need, behavior, state, constraint, platform, and known non-fit—not only industry or visual keywords.
2. Prefer applicable current entries with primary-source and consumer-authority links.
3. If no confident match exists, abstain, broaden the query once, and disclose any fallback. Do not force a nearest aesthetic stereotype.
4. Verify freshness and the consumer’s effective canon before recommending use.
5. Carry the source ID/revision and evidence boundary into the Reuse Assessment.

## Maintenance

- A new upstream revision proposes an index update. It does not silently replace consumer canon.
- Superseded entries remain findable with a forward link so old decisions retain lineage.
- Remove copied normative text when an authoritative link and concise retrieval summary are sufficient.
- Add an entry only when it improves cross-task decisions. One-off research can remain in the task evidence packet.
